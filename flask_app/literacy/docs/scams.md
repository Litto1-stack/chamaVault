# Investment and M-Pesa scam red flags

- Guaranteed high returns (e.g. “50% sure”)
- Pressure to pay today
- Asking for M-Pesa PIN or OTP
- Unlicensed brokers or “account managers”
- Fee to unlock profits or reverse a transfer

Verify CMA / SASRA / CBK licences. Real M-Pesa and ChamaVault never ask for your PIN by SMS or chat.
