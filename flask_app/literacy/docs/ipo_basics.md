# Public share offers and IPOs (Kenya)

A company may sell shares to the public through a prospectus supervised under Capital Markets Authority (CMA) rules, often listing on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE).

Buying shares means ownership risk: prices can fall; dividends are not guaranteed; you may wait years before selling at a profit — or sell at a loss.

Before any offer (including well-known brands):
- Read the official prospectus, not social media rumours.
- Confirm the offer and intermediaries are CMA-authorised.
- Match money you commit to a long time horizon (often multi-year).
- Never use rent, school fees, or emergency cash to buy shares.
- Past company success does not guarantee your personal return.
