# Regulated savings and investment channels (Kenya)

- **CMA** — Capital Markets Authority: unit trusts, fund managers, brokers, public offers.
- **SASRA** — deposit-taking SACCOs.
- **CBK** — banks, payment providers, and some securities (e.g. government securities channels).

Money market funds and unit trusts: returns vary; check fees and liquidity; use licensed managers only.

Treasury bills and bonds: government securities with defined tenors; still subject to interest-rate and inflation risk; buy through official channels.

Digital loans and “guaranteed investment” apps: high cost or scam risk. Prefer chama/SACCO discipline and licensed products.
