Frequently asked questions

Do I need a bank account or an ID?

No. Your phone number and a PIN are enough to join, because your group vouches for you.

Is real money moving in this version?

No. M-Pesa, card and SMS are simulated so you can try everything safely. They sit behind service classes so real Daraja and Africa's Talking can be connected later.

Can the treasurer change an entry?

No one can edit or delete an entry. The treasurer can add a correction with a written reason; the original stays visible as CORRECTED and everyone gets an SMS.

How do I know the ledger has not been tampered with?

Open Verify. It recomputes every hash in order and tells you the first record that doesn't match.

How is my credit score worked out?

Early +15, on time +10, loan installment on schedule +25, a 3-payment streak +20, late -15, missed -40, loan default -100. See My score for the full explanation.

What is the difference between flat and reducing-balance interest?

Flat adds one fixed percentage to the amount borrowed. Reducing balance charges interest each month only on what you still owe, so it costs less if you repay early.

How does the merry-go-round work?

Everyone pays the same pot amount each month. Once all have paid, the treasurer pays the whole pot to that month's member, and the turn moves on.

Is the election really secret?

The app stores that you voted and, separately, a vote for a candidate, and never shows a link between them. This demo database is not hardened against someone with direct database access.
