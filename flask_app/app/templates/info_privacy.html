Privacy

This is a demo. Please don't enter real personal or financial information.

What we keep

  • Your name, phone number, optional email, and a hashed PIN (the PIN itself is never stored or logged).
  • Your ledger entries, loans, votes, score events, personal savings goals, and the SMS messages the system "sent".
  • Vaulti questions you ask in this demo session (processed to answer; not sold).

What we never keep

  • Card numbers, expiry dates or CVVs: they are checked for format and discarded.
  • ID documents or bank details.

Sharing

Group members can see the group ledger; that visibility is the point of the product.

If you enable them, email may go through Resend, voice through Cognaria, and Vaulti LLM answers through Together AI (for an Adaption-trained model). Those providers process the content needed to deliver the feature. Nothing is sold for advertising.
