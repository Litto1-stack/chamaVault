How ChamaVault works

M-Pesa moves the money. ChamaVault proves it.

  1. Join with your phone number. The treasurer invites you by SMS. No ID upload, no bank account. You pick a 4-digit PIN.
  2. Agree the rules. Monthly amount, due day, daily late penalty, loan interest (flat or reducing), who approves loans and whether a guarantor is needed, all set in one place.
  3. Pay and record. Members pay by M-Pesa or card; the treasurer records cash with a PIN. Every payment becomes a ledger entry and everyone gets an SMS.
  4. Prove it. Each entry is chained to the one before it with a SHA-256 hash. One tap on Verify re-checks the whole history and points to the first broken record.
  5. Grow your score. Pay early and on time to build a chama credit score, then use it to borrow from your own group.
