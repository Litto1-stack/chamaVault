What is in the app

  • Tamper-evident ledger: append-only, hash-chained, verifiable, with visible corrections.
  • Payments: M-Pesa (simulated), card (demo, never stored), cash recorded by the treasurer, and a phone-matched M-Pesa webhook.
  • Loans with your rules: flat or reducing interest, optional guarantor, member vote or treasurer approval.
  • Elections: secret-ballot treasurer elections run by the signatories.
  • Safety: quiet SMS alerts to two signatories for unusual activity.
  • Reminders: day-before, due-morning and due-evening SMS.
  • Credit score and Vaulti: a score from your real payment record and a bilingual money coach.
  • Tools: printable statements with verification codes, ledger CSV export, calculators, and an installable app.
